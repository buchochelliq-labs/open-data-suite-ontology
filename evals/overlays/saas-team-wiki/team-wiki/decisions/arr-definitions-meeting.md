# ARR definitions

The council discussed [[Annual recurring revenue (ARR)]] again. Finance wants MRR × 12, sales
wants the value of signed contracts. No decision: it moves to the Q1 2027 metrics review,
owned by the CFO and the VP Sales.

Until then, show both figures side by side with their labels. (Several people noted that most
internal decks already use the finance figure.)
