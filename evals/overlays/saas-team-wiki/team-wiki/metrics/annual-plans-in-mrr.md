# Why annual plans count at a twelfth in MRR

The 2025 board deck was built on [[mrr_legacy]], which counted an annual plan's full price
in its signing month. January is when most annual renewals land, so January 2025 MRR looked
38% higher than it really was, and February looked like a crash.

In the January 2026 planning review finance changed the rule for [[Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)]]:
annual plans count at one twelfth of their price each month. `fct_mrr` follows the new rule.
